学习 Wardley Mapping 的粗略笔记

2024-11-30 · 阅读原文

在我持续撰写一本关于工程战略的书的过程中，我终于到了需要把“Wardley Mapping”从“考虑是否纳入”的主题，转变为“决定纳入或不纳入”的阶段。第一步就是要深入理解它的工作原理。这与我已经在工作中断断续续使用了 15 年多的 使用系统建模来完善策略 相当不同，但我会认真尝试一下。

我的起点是读完了 David Anderson 的《The Value Flywheel Effect》，并尝试过几次但未能成功读完 Simon Wardley 在 Medium 上连载的《Wardley Maps》。让我们看看进展如何。

  1. Markus pointed me towards his excellent list of Wardley mapping resources
  2. What Is Wardley Mapping? by Mike Lamb is a quick, very high-level introduction to the concepts.
  3. The Easiest Way to Do Wardley Mapping (A Tutorial) is a good first starting point.Within this example, there are two templates to work from: lists & value chain template and Wardley map template. You can then use the template in whatever diagramming tool you prefer, the video uses Miro, and I used OmniGraffle.It also links to this table for determining stage of a given activity within a Wardley Map, and this table describing opportunities. Both of these are useful in various steps of creating/refining a Wardley Map.
  4. How to read a Wardley Map. Bit of a repeat of above.
  5. The Problem with Wardley Mapping. Talks about the various ways that people often get stuck using Wardley Maps: taking “all or nothing” approaches to both method and scope. Rooted in this article, Nobody Cares About Your Precious Framework.First, it describes the number of “Wardley mapping practices” and how it’s valuable to simplify initially. Specifically, start by just building lists of users and needs, as opposed to trying everything all at once. Second, it describes how users can have many needs, there might be three or four users each with ten needs, which gets overwhelming. To solve that, focus on doing one user and one need, and then expand from there to prove out the value.
  6. Top 5 Wardley Mapping Tools in 2024 on YouTube, and also available in blog post. Of these, particularly interested in damonsk/onlinewardleymaps which seems like a Graphviz approach, and reminds me a bit of my experiment with lethain/systems that I build for systems modeling.That said, I’ll probably give Mapkeep a try first. My issue with systems modeling tooling is primarily that it’s very confusing to use, and Mapkeep seems like it might have the lowest overhead to usage of these Wardley mapping tools.
  7. Situation Normal, Everything Must Change by Simon Wardley on YouTube. This is a good overview of Wardley mapping, although a bit surface level. A good first watch.
  8. Wardley Mapping Part 1-4 by Simon Wardley on YouTube. A more detailed introduction to the same content as the Situation Normal, Everything Must Change talk above. The first two are a bit redundant with some of the prior talks, but the third and fourth are additive and quite useful.
  9. Mapping Your Stack by Adrian Cockroft. Much of this is repeat of the above, but around 22:20 it goes into mapping a concrete example that I found quite interesting.
  10. Wardley Maps for Software Developers by Markus Harrer. Most helpfully, it gets into concrete maps quickly, and walks through a number of distinct examples.
  11. At this point, I signed up for a free Mapkeep, and started mapping.
  12. My first project was this 衡量开发者体验、基准测试，以及提供改进理论。, which gave me the opportunity to play around with a simple map and the evolution of that map over the past four years.At this time, that’s still just a draft post, but was still a good opportunity for mapping practice. I also think it would have been much harder to form and articulate my point of view without the map, which is a good signal.
  13. Next, I did Gitlab 战略的 Wardley Mapping (e.g. why focus on bundling all-in versus Github’s strategy of supporting best-in-breed unbundling). This was quick, and instructive. It’s also giving me a better sense of what sort of problems Wardley mapping is useful for exploring (and which it isn’t so useful at digging into).

到了这个阶段，我已经相当自在地打开 Mapkeeper 并开始绘制地图了。这并不是说我不能再提升——我肯定还有很大进步空间——但我的感觉是，这主要取决于多做地图练习，而不是依赖某种顿悟式的神奇领悟。

Wardley Mapping 还捆绑了很多其他内容，我也认为其中不少非常有用，但对我而言，并没有我在这里重点关注的那些部分那么重要。例如，Wardley 提出的 doctrine 和 gameplay 概念都很棒，但我通常会把这类东西看作“某一种特定的战略”，而不是一个完全不同的类别。这并不是对 Wardley 框架的否定，只是我找到了一个对我当前关注的问题来说更有用的不同视角。
