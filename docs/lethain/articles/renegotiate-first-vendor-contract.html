重新谈判你的第一个供应商合同

2020-11-14 · 阅读原文

不久前我写了一篇自建还是采购。，讨论了如何评估供应商工具与自建内部方案的利弊。那篇文章的核心观点是，我认为大多数公司应该更多地采用供应商工具。不过，与其给出如何挑选供应商工具的建议，我觉得对大多数工程师来说，更有帮助的话题是如何重新谈判公司已有的即将到期的合同。

在大型、成熟的公司里，你会有供应商管理或采购团队可以合作进行谈判，他们通常会非常认真地争取达成有利交易。根据合同规模，他们可能会启动涉及多家竞争供应商的流程，整个过程一般需要花费一些时间才能完成。但如果你在一家小公司，很可能某天上班时突然意识到，必须在合同两周后到期前重新谈判。你可能几乎得不到任何支持，这对第一次毫无章法地参与谈判的工程师或工程经理来说，会让人相当紧张。

供应商通常只看重你能提供的几样东西：

  1. Money, preferably guaranteed
  2. A long-term commitment, preferably exclusive
  3. Help meeting their internal goals, including hitting their quarterly quotas and buying higher-margin services (ever wonder why they’d rather reduce the total price than remove that training fee?)
  4. Sometimes timing of payments, for example a small company with a capital intensive business will really value upfront payment over on-demand payment
  5. Sometimes co-marketing, if you’d help appeal to their future customers or investors

另一方面，在重新谈判合同时，你一般希望从供应商那里获得的是：

  1. A volume-based discount so that as you spend more, their rates should decrease. You should never pay the same rate as your spend increases unless you’re confident you’ve negotiated the vendor’s margin away in a previous round and that they haven’t subsequently improved their margins
  2. A commitment length that aligns with your potential timeline to consider other vendors. If you’re confident you won’t reconsider within two years, then maybe two years is fine. Generally contracts longer than one year should be undertaken with due care
  3. Discount based on service and delivery issues during the previous contract. If they had frequent outages, this should be reflected in the contract to some extent, in the sense of pricing in that risk. You should never frame this around the actual contract commitments, almost all software SLAs commitments are very generous to the provider, but instead around the harm and inconvenience you’ve experienced due to their incidents

我推荐的一般做法是：

  1. Align with internal users of the vendor that you should continue paying for it. A surprising number of vendors stop getting used without anyone really recognizing it
  2. Align with internal users of the vendor on any major issues you’re having with them. Maybe it’s API instability, rate limits, slow responses, etc. The renegotiation process is the only time you have real leverage to get these addressed
  3. Before you go into your first discussion with the vendor, talk through the negotiation game plan with whoever is going to sign the contract. Always have a plan!
  4. In particular, figure out the volume discount and any poor service discounts you want
  5. Go into the first negotiation and work to get good numbers according to that discussion. Emphasize that you’re taking the lead on the contract
  6. Once you get written numbers back from the vendor, then shift the negotiation to the contract signer, and say that regrettably they’ve taken over the negotiation from you
  7. Have the contract signer push the vendor a bit more on pricing
  8. Sign the contract

这绝不是最有效的谈判策略，但它可靠、省时间，而且总是能为你省下一笔可观的费用。随着你做得越来越多，你会逐渐掌握更 nuanced 的方法，也能找到获得更深折扣的办法。但在第一次合同谈判时不用太担心这些：拿到一个合理的成果，然后继续前进。对工程师或工程经理来说，花太多时间在合同谈判上的机会成本很高；同样，完全不在合同谈判上花时间所带来的财务成本也相当高。

注意，这和 처음与供应商签订合同时的谈判方法也有所不同。后者实际上发生得少得多，因为你通常每年都会续约，而初始合同只会谈一次。
