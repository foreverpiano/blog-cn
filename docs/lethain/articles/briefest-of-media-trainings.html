最简短的媒体培训。

2018-06-24 · 阅读原文

当我在 Digg v4 的架构与开发流程 工作时，有幸从 Christine 那里获得了五分钟的媒体培训。这次短暂的培训深深印在了我的脑海中，从那以后我经常重复这些内容。这周再次分享时，我意识到或许应该把它写下来！

与媒体交谈的三条规则：

  1. Answer the question you want to be asked. If someone asks a very difficult or challenging question, reframe it into one that you’re comfortable answering. Don’t accept a question’s implicit framing, but instead take the opportunity to frame it yourself. Don’t Think of An Elephant is a phenomenal, compact guide to framing issues.
  2. Stay positive. Negative stories can be very compelling. They are quite risky too! As an interviewee, find a positive framing and stick to it. Especially true when it comes to competitors and controversy.
  3. Speak in threes. Narrow your message down to three concise points, make them your refrain, and continue to reference back into your three speaking points.

就是这些！简明、紧凑，而十年后的今天我仍在使用它们，并从中不断学习。
