摩擦不等于速度。

2024-03-15 · 阅读原文

当你开车行驶在路上，可能会觉得有点闷，于是决定把车窗摇下来。这时空气会灌进来，风声会变大，行进的感觉也会更强烈。引擎为了维持原来的速度，会开始更费力——声音也更响。每一种感官都会告诉你，你正在开得更快，但其实摇下车窗增加了车辆的空气阻力，你实际上开得更慢了。或者至少，你正在消耗更多燃油来维持同样的速度。

第一段所说的内容你可能早就知道，但它仍然是我见过高压下的高管最常犯的一类推理错误。如果你不确定如何取得进展，那么从情绪上讲，用“行动”来替代“没有进展”会让人感觉好得多，但在现实中，你的情况其实更糟了。

用几个例子来具体说明：

  • Many companies realize that their monolithic codebase is slowing them down. It’s easy to decide to migrate from your monolith to services to “solve” this problem, but without a clear service architecture, most attempts take a long time without improving on the underlying issues. That’s because an effective service migration requires the same skill to operate an effective monolith: good technical design.However, the microservice migration itself provides a reassuring sensation of progress, delaying for a year or two the realization that you’re in roughly the same place that you started in.
  • When your engineering organization doesn’t seem to be shipping enough software, an easy solution is to rollout a new development process. For example, you might say that an ineffective team needs to start following the scrum development technique.In rare case that the team has never considered any approach to organize their work, then this might well help. In most cases, this will just paper over whatever problem is causing the slow down, creating an appearance of progress that’ll quickly fade away.
  • It’s common for new executives to rollout their preferrenced knowledge base, e.g. Notion or Confluence or whatnot, operating from the belief that the tool itself is the fundamental driver of an effective knowledge base.This will create months of work to move to a new knowledge base, but generally does not improve the underlying knowledge being managed. Poorly managed knowledge bases are always related to incentives and culture, not checkbox ready feature lists like “effective search.”

这里的模式通常是：由资深领导者基于直觉做出的决策，成功标准不清晰，把“行动”当作“进展”的有效代理，同时又太忙而无暇反思先前的行动是否达成了预期目标。这种做法会被当作“领导力”，也确实和培养领导风格中描述的某些领导风格有相似之处，但它始终是比其他可行选项更差的策略。

如果你看到有人采用这种策略，善意地指出来是对他们真正的帮助。如果他们对这类反馈不感兴趣，那你就学到了一件重要的事：他们更在意的是表演领导力的样子，而不是自己工作的实际影响。

需要补充一点的是，当你完全陷入停滞时，减少摩擦其实没那么重要。在那种情况下，Travis Kalanick 那句经典的话就很合适：“恐惧是疾病，狂飙是解药。” 盲目狂乱的行动固然比不上深思熟虑的追求，但总比悄无声息地走向那个良夜要好。
