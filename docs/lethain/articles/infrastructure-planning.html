基础设施规划：用户、基线与时间框架

2018-12-09 · 阅读原文

技术基础设施永远没有完结的时候。系统进程总是可以减少开销，或是打包到更少的机器上运行。数据可以被更快地获取，同时以更低的每TB成本进行存储。系统设计可以进一步拉大故障与用户影响之间的差距。传输层可以变得更加安全。

可投入的项目种类之多令人应接不暇。总有新的技术可以采用，或是迁移：解决技术债务唯一可扩展的办法。：Docker、Kubernetes、Envoy、GKE、HTTP/2、GraphQL、gRPC、Spark、Flink、Rust、Go、Elixir，这些仅仅是你可选方案的冰山一角。再加上云厂商之间的竞争，变化的速度相当惊人。

面对如此广阔的问题域和如此多的可能解决方案，我有时会发现很难为基础设施团队提供工作优先级的指导。最初我以为这是因为自己在某些方面缺乏深度，但慢慢意识到，团队自己为工作排优先级同样困难：选项实在太多了。

几年前，我整理了一套基础设施规划框架，我将其称为基础设施：介于成本中心与自我陶醉之间，它在一定复杂度下运行良好，但最近已无法提供我们所需的指导程度。因此，在过去几个月里，我迭代出了第二个框架：用户、基线与时间框架。

让我们深入了解最初和新的框架吧！

基础设施的五个属性

当我们制定第一个基础设施规划框架时，希望能提供更清晰指导的问题包括：

  1. security and reliability first, then everything else: we wanted every single team to have a consistent culture and approach of prioritizing security and reliability work before all other work,
  2. prioritizing important but not critical work: there was enough critical work that a successful framework would provide support for shifting from a short-term focus and towards long-term investment,
  3. resourcing the long tail of opportunities: some organizations ignore cost efficiency and latency concerns until they find themselves sipping coffee in the burning kitchen of meme, and we wanted to make sure we funneled a small but consistent quantity of resources to these areas.

我们的解决方案是一份按优先级排序的基础设施属性清单，我们会对每一项都投入一些工作，但对排序靠前的投入更多：

  1. Security is more than “locking things down”, it’s streamlining work so that the same tasks can be accomplished more securely with the same level of effort.
  2. Reliability is uptime, harvest, and yield, hardening your interfaces, and ensuring your oncall load is low enough that alerts are actioned instead of ignored.
  3. Productivity is making the engineers supported by your team more effective through potent primitives and abstractions more potent (some would argue 无服务器架构：守门机制与利润空间 is such an improvement over service-oriented computing), or by offering entirely new capabilities.
  4. Efficiency is providing the same service at a lower cost. This can be reducing maintenance overhead to spend more time on features, and it can also be better picking more appropriate AWS or GCP instances.
  5. Latency is both reducing time to reach your servers (through points of presence, an active-active multi-region/datacenter topology, or connection pooling with established connections), and also time within your stack (via better load balancing strategies, request tracing to find latency hot spots, or optimizing database queries).

这不是一个完美的框架，但在规划会议中相当有用。特别是它很好地平衡了对内部和外部用户的关注，避免了基础设施团队在优先级排序中常见的“自肥”陷阱。

然而，这个框架无法回答一些重要问题：

  1. Security is ranked above reliability, but it’s clear that some reliability work is more important than some security work. How do we make these trade offs?
  2. Within a given category, there is an infinite amount of possible work. How do we narrow our focus to remain aligned with our users’ needs? Also, which users needs?
  3. How will work to improve lower ranked priorioties, such as latency, ever get prioritized?
  4. How should we prioritize between these two users’ security asks?

于是我们重新开始，基于之前行之有效的部分构建新的框架。

关于创造力的题外话

在进入下一个框架之前，先简单谈谈创造力。我们作为领导者所做的最有价值的工作之一，就是避免将权衡视为不可避免，而是将其视为偶然的结果。

变通方法比比皆是。

凭借专业知识和创造力，我们常常能够“两者兼得”。我把这称为“拿双份薪水”，这也是为什么你应该避免团队过度忙碌的重要理由之一：疲惫的人是没有创造力的。

用户、基线与时间框架

当我们坐下来迭代基础设施优先级框架时，我们特别希望改进的地方是：

  1. Most planning periods should prioritize a healthy mix of security, reliability, productivity, efficiency and latency work.
  2. Project mix should be reactive to our problems—if developer productivity decreased significantly, it should prioritize more investment there—and to our users’ needs.
  3. Avoid overfitting on our current capabilities, especially SLAs. The platform we aspire offer five years from now should be an order of magnitude improvement on what we offer today.
  4. Protecting our ability to invest into the sorts of work we’ll need to undertake to accomplish those order of magnitude improvements.
  5. Provides structure for explaining our tradeoffs to our users.

我们花了一些时间反思前一个版本中哪些做得好、哪些做得不好，以及那些改进目标。从这些反思中，我们开发出的系统是：

  • Baselines are set for every infrastructure property: security, reliability, productivity, efficiency and latency. These are in effect the service level objectives we commit to providing. (目标与基线.)
  • Timeframes are the different eras across which we ensure we’ll maintain our baselines. What work do we need to maintain our baselines this year? What about next year? Five years out?
  • Users are the folks, internal and external, who we enable and support. Can we group our users into cohorts with similar needs? Can we provide a breakdown of how much time we want to invest into which cohort for the next year? Two years?

现在，我们以用户需求清单开始规划周期，然后加入为在合适的时间框架内维持基线所需的项目。当用户想了解为什么我们在做基础设施工作而不是其他项目时，我们可以明确地将我们的投入与业务价值联系起来。

虽然这不是一个完美的工具，但我发现它是解释优先级和权衡的一种强大而有效的方式。

在制定这些指导原则的早期，最有趣的问题之一是基础设施团队是否应该将彼此列为客户。

对于整个组织来说，我认为答案通常是否定的。基础设施组织的价值衡量标准，是它所能带来的未来产品开发速度的折现，而这个模型强调的是，所有自我投入都是为了提高未来的产品速度。

相反，在基础设施组织内部的团队层面，我认为非常重要的是要将其他基础设施团队视为你的用户。这一点特别重要，因为好的基础设施产品应该是提供可穿透的抽象层的，它们建立在其他基础设施产品之上，让人们能够为自己的项目选择合适的生产力与属性权衡。

资源分配

当人们阅读这类框架时，通常第一个非常合理的问题是：“你们真的这么做吗？”在这种情况下，我们正在结束我所在团队的最新一轮规划流程，这里写的内容是我们实际做法的一个相当准确、略微抽象且稍加理想化的版本。

唯一省略的部分是，我们还会叠加一个简单的资源分配框架，以确保我们投入到可持续可运维的系统上，并支持那些能以战术工作无法做到的方式改变我们基线的长期项目。

该资源分配框架的当前版本是：

  • User asks (40%) - our largest chunk of work goes directly towards users asks (as prioritized by users, baselines and timeframes). Our biggest bucket of work is immediately improving our users lives.
  • Platform quality (30%) - maintaining our current and future baselines. This is also our budget to reduce toil in operating our systems. This isn’t the only time we prioritize baseline work, sometimes those baselines are user asks as well, this is instead the minimum amount of time we spend shoring up the baselines.
  • Key initiatives (30%) - each half we select one to three very large projects at the engineering “group” level (in this case, a bit over one hundred engineers) that solve an entire category of user asks or provide a generational leap in hitting our baselines. We scope the projects to what we can finish in six months; then we finish that work in six months. This protects our ability to innovate, while avoiding never-ending projects.

这个框架非常简单，但我发现它运作良好。特别是它确保了在资源分配过程中会提出正确的问题，而这正是资源分配和规划讨论的最终目标。

我非常好奇想听听大家的情况——你们的团队如何进行基础设施规划？哪些方法对你们有效？哪些无效？你们面临的最大挑战是什么？
