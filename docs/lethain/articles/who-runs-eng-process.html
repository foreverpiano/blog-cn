谁来负责工程流程？

2023-04-03 · 阅读原文

Uber 曾运行过一个名为 DUCK Review 的技术规格评审流程。“DUCK”并不代表任何含义——它是故意设计的一个非缩写——但除此之外，它是一个相当典型的评审流程。我刚加入时，我们每周会评审一到两个规格。随着评审请求量不断增加，六个月后，从提交评审请求到收到反馈之间出现了为一到两周的延迟。又过了一年，由于没有足够带宽处理所有 incoming 的规格，这个流程被废弃了。这对我来说是一次很有启发性的经历，因为 DUCK Review 确实提供了非常有价值的反馈，但最终还是失败了，因为它的运营成本超过了我们愿意为这些反馈付出的代价。

在我管理生涯的早期，我认为大多数流程失败是因为它们对当前问题的解决不够充分。我以为如果一个流程的结果不佳，那是因为流程需要更多的细微差别和复杂性。此后我通过设计那些考虑周全却依然失败的流程，学到的教训是：好的流程是在质量和开销之间有意识的权衡。比如，我们常看到一种快速运行但反馈质量较低的代码评审流程，要求在 pull request 提交后两个工作小时内完成评审。同样常见的是那种非常繁琐、高质量的流程，最终大家因为太慢而忽略它，包括前面提到的 DUCK Review 的故事。

要确定你的团队需要何种程度的流程复杂度，你必须先弄清楚谁来负责运行这些流程，然后从这个答案反向推导出你的组织能够持续支撑的流程复杂度。为了帮助你思考这个问题，我将逐一分析：

  • What is the typical progression for companies across patterns?
  • What are the pros and cons of these patterns?
  • How do you operate the baseline pattern successfully?
  • How do you deal with the budgeting realities?
  • How should you navigate the trend cycle?

读完之后，你会对当今组织中应该由谁来负责流程、什么时候应该考虑切换模式，以及在人员配置上避免最常见错误的一些建议，形成清晰的认识。

这是 O'Reilly 出版的《The Engineering Executive’s Primer》中的一章，未经编辑。

典型的模式演进

我观察到组织在运行工程范围和公司范围的流程时，通常会采用五种常见模式。不管你今天使用哪种模式，很可能三年前你在用另一种；如果你增长迅速，那么三年后很可能又会采用第三种模式。没有绝对正确的模式，只有最适合你当前约束条件的模式。（有一种模式是错误的，那就是业务单元模式，它很少能运行得特别好。）

早期创业公司

所有新成立的公司都采用我所说的 Early Startup 模式：要么由创始人或职能高管为自己的职能做某件事，要么这件事就不发生。我在 Calm 担任 CTO 的第一年，公司没有共享的人员流程，我用一个 spreadsheet 运行工程的绩效评审流程。它需要被完成，而我处于可以去做的位置。这就是极小规模公司的工作方式，通常会持续到前 30-50 名员工入职，之后他们会转向稍微更有结构化的做法。

Baseline 模式

下一个阶段是 Baseline 模式，大约在 50 名员工左右开始采用，并且是大多数公司的最终模式。公司范围的流程（例如公司规划、绩效管理和预算）由中央 People（即人力资源）职能负责，而工程范围的流程（例如 Tech Spec Review、Incident Review 和开发者生产力调查）则由工程师和工程经理负责。没有专门的角色来支持工程流程，对公司流程针对工程的专门化程度也有限。

专业化工程角色

当公司规模超过 200 名工程师时，工程范围的流程往往会消耗足够多的时间，以至于受益于全职支持。这通常首先发生在 Incident Review 或 Tech Spec Review 上，但根据你公司的具体情况，任何一个流程都可能第一个变得足够昂贵而需要专人。我称其为 Specialized Engineering Roles 模式，因为大多数公司通过建立一个新职能来解决这个问题，通常是 Technical Program Management 或 Engineering Operations。

公司嵌入式角色

在许多科技公司中，工程是最大的职能，因此会比业务其他部分更早达到某些规模点。为了确保流程在所有这些扩展需求下仍能正常运作，往往会导致工程从公司默认的招聘、绩效管理等机制中分离出来，从而有专职的招聘人员和 People 团队成员与工程一起工作。我称其为 Company Embedded Roles 模式。

除了纯粹的规模因素外，工程还有其他几个因素会产生特别复杂的需求，并支持向这种模式的转变：

  • Dual-sided career ladder across engineers and engineering managers, whereas other functions typically have only one
  • Often includes one or more specialized roles along the lines of Developer Relations, Technical Program Management, Quality Engineering, or Security Engineering
  • Engineers and engineering managers tend to be slower and more expensive to hire and retain than most other functions
  • Long ramp time from starting to becoming highly productive, which places a higher value on retention

业务单元本地化

最后是 Business Unit Local 模式，即工程向每个业务单元的领导层汇报，不再有一个领导者对整个工程负责。这通常会导致工程回归由中央职能运行的标准化流程，或者接受不一致性，让每个业务单元的工程组织自行寻找前进的道路。

各种模式的优缺点

现在我们已经介绍了公司经历这些流程管理模式的典型序列，我想更深入地探讨这些模式本身的优缺点：

  • Early Startup: when you start a new company, it’s just one or more founders. Early hires will almost always focus on building a small product to validate the market or run the business day to day, and generally won’t include anyone focused purely on process. This makes sense, because processes are about coordinating large groups, and the company is only a handful of people at this point. Any process that you want to run needs to be run by the founders, or managers hired by those founders.Companies usually exit this pattern because there is impactful process they want to run, often initially this is performance management, but they feel they’re too busy to run it with the current team.Pros: low cost, low overhead, few downsides at small scale beacuse most process is focused on supporting large teamsCons: often valuable stuff doesn’t happen because everyone is too busy, sometimes quality of process is very low due to limited time or experience running that process
  • Baseline: Engineering-scoped processes are run by engineers or engineering managers, and company-scoped processes are run by a centralized function, usually in People or Human Resources. Most companies stay in the baseline pattern for an extended period of time, with perhaps one small exception for a dedicated recruiter for engineering hires. It typically works well through 100+ engineers.One of the reasons this works significantly better than the early startup pattern is that identifies a clear team responsible for selecting vendors (e.g. Greenhouse for hiring needs, Lattice for performance management). These tools are not perfect, but they’re significantly better than a spreadsheet, which is the default tool when seven managers are independently trying to run a decentralized process.Pros: some modest specialization to allow Engineering to focus on engineering rather than foundational company process, complexity of HR tasks tend to outstrip engineering manager depth by this phase (e.g. managing visas), unified systems allow executive team to inspect across functions rather than just in their own functionCons: outcomes are highly dependent on quality of these centralized functions, changing company-scoped processes becomes challenging
  • Specialized Engineering Roles: increase efficiency in Engineering-scoped processes by hiring dedicated, specialized roles to fulfill that work. Typically this is hiring either an Engineering Operations or Technical Program Management team. The first areas of investment are usually organizational planning, incident management, and 运行你的工程入职项目.Pros: specialized roles generally introduce significant efficiency, offering a better skill set for the task to be done at a lower salary point. Introducing these roles is usually energizing for the individuals no longer doing the work. Specialists who are able to focus on a given process often make significant improvements in that processCons: While it is more efficient, it’s usually more expensive as these roles introduce additional headcount. It’s 引入 SRE、TPM 及其他专业化角色 like engineering operations or technical program management. Specialized roles often freeze a company in a given way of working, whereas engineers are incentivized to eliminate a process, the specialist is incentivized to improve it
  • Company Embedded Roles: Engineering’s needs for company-scoped processes reach a sufficient scale or sufficiently diverge from standard operating procedure such that support functions like People and Recruiting hire individuals specifically to support engineering. This usually happens one function at a time, often starting with Recruiting, followed by People and Finance.While it will often happen first for Engineering, this pattern will repeat for other sizable organizations in the company, for example you’ll frequently see a setup where Engineering and Sales have dedicated embeds, and the rest of the organization work with a centralized team. Within a sufficiently large company, every function will have dedicated support.Pros: empowers Engineering to customize process and approach. Builds stronger relationships across functionsCons: expensive to operate. Quality is heavily dependent on the embedded individuals
  • Business Unit Local: each business unit in a company has its own, separate engineering function. This split typically occurs when an organization becomes large enough to have multiple meaningful business units, and there’s a perception that standardization across engineering in those business units is unnecessary or unnecessarily expensive. For example, one business unit might require HITRUST compliance, whereas the others do not, which might lead to significantly different engineering tradeoffs.Sometimes this pattern is adopted but one business unit is so much larger than the others, that it becomes the defacto engineering hub for the wider company, including leading cross-organizational developer tooling and infrastructure investments.Pros: aligns engineering with business priorities within each business unitCon: engineering process and strategy often gets stuck wherever it was when the split first happened. Future changes require consensus across many engineering leaders. Investments across engineering become difficult to prioritize

所有这些模式都有缺陷，但都值得考虑。话虽如此，如果你在没有任何额外上下文的情况下让我选择一个，我总是会推荐 baseline 模式。它通常已经足够好，而且最容易管理。

运作 Baseline 模式

我所说的 Baseline 是运行流程最常见的方法，由中央 People 组织负责公司范围的流程，由工程师和工程经理混合负责工程范围的流程。

它常见有几个原因：

  • It’s the default scenario: simply don’t hire any specialized roles or embed support directly with engineering and you’re at the baseline
  • It’s relatively simple: you don’t have to learn to hire or manage anything new, and it doesn’t require treating engineering differently from other functions
  • It appears cheaper: when you propose moving to another pattern, it’s usually framed as a way to free up existing capacity to focus on higher value work, but that requires hiring additional roles to support the pattern, such as Technical Program Managers. That makes it more expensive from a budgeting perspective, even if it’s meaningfully more effective, and most budgeting discussions are cost-driven rather than effectiveness-driven

它常见的第四个原因是：它确实运行得相当好！我工作过的每家公司，包括 Uber 和 Stripe，都在这个模式下走了很远，远超一百名工程师才离开它。我个人认为许多公司切换到其他模式太早了，往往是因为他们在复制之前就职的、规模大得多的公司的 playbook。

如果你被诱惑要从 Baseline 切换出去，而且公司规模还在一百名工程师以下，我建议你先重点关注以下几件事，尝试让当前模式为你发挥作用：

  • Rotate the work. Calm has had great success with six month rotations, where individuals serve in one capacity (e.g. running Incident Review) for six months before swapping off. We increase continuity by having two people serve together in each capacity with overlapping but offset periods of service
  • Make the process interesting for the people running it. If someone is leading your Incident Review, also give them exposure to the company’s wider planning process as you factor in reliability projects. If someone is running your Tech Spec Review, pull them into your annual revision of 撰写工程战略. You should go beyond service, folks should feel that these are unique learning opportunities to work with senior leadership
  • Ensure your promotion rubrics, or your promotions if you don’t have a rubric yet, value this kind of work. You can even experiment with the rubric strongly preferring that engineers serve a term in one of your processes before moving into Staff-plus or managerial roles. People are smart and drift towards the work that you value
  • Make sure to call out the individuals doing the work behind your processes, particularly 打造高效工程组织的会议体系

虽然没有保证，但我的亲身经历是，做这些相对小的调整就能让 Baseline 模式良好运转，并且能比你预期的更长时间地持续有效。

应对预算现实

虽然在不同模式之间迁移存在运营上的挑战，但这些挑战大多只有在事后回顾时才明显。相反，从 Early Startup 转向 Baseline 或从 Baseline 转向 Specialized Engineering Roles 的最大障碍是预算影响。即使你能证明三个 Technical Program Manager 会显著提升工程的影响力——这很可能正确，但你很难用 spreadsheet 证明——大多数预算流程还是锚定在相对美元数字上，而不是绝对影响上。

深入来看，面临的问题是：

  • Most companies that successfully shift up the patterns’ cost curve do so when they are rapidly growing. Such companies are comfortable with rapidly growing headcount costs, and are generally tolerant of adding roles to support that growth. In all other circumstances, companies generally aren’t tolerant of adding new roles and structures, even if they would likely increase efficiency. In the latter scenarios, you’re much more likely to hear folks talk about “doing more with less” than “designing our organization to operate efficiently,” and it’s very hard to make efficiency-based arguments with an executive team in a cost reduction mindset
  • Many companies operate to a portfolio allocation target across departments, e.g. 20% on General & Administrative (G&A), 30% to Sales & Marketing (S&M), and 50% to Research & Development (R&D). Most pattern shifts will eat into the G&A budget, increasing G&A costs and preventing other planned G&A hiring. This generally means that many of your peer executives will be resistant to the proposal
  • A surprising number of budgeting processes operate in headcount rather than budget, which makes it hard to intuitively express ideas like “replacing two high cost Staff engineers with three mid-level Technical Program Managers.” Headcount-anchored budgeting processes portray the later as more expensive than the former, even though it’s not. It’s possible to push through this confusion, but it imposes an ongoing communication cost

每一个这些顾虑都可以通过规划、耐心和信念来克服，但你必须自己决定是否值得这么做。我的一般建议是坚持使用 Baseline，除非收入或人头数正在显著增长，否则避免沿着模式成本曲线向上迁移。

应对潮流周期

存在许多流程潮流，例如行业曾一度痴迷于 Agile：我曾经和一位职位名称是 Agiletect 的人共事。同样，在谁来负责流程，以及演进流程是高度有影响力的、必不可少的工作，还是无聊、枯燥、不会帮助你晋升的“glue work”上，也存在潮流。在我自己的职业生涯中，我看到过从“技术管理”到“以人为中心的管理”的重大转变，以及同样突然的反向反应，从“以人为中心的管理”转向“管理是开销”。

如果我对每一种潮流都照单全收，那么我就会给我领导的团队带来持续的动荡，我们永远无法在任何一种工作方式上变得特别擅长。这并不是说潮流没有意义，每个潮流背后总有某些真实的东西。在行业 downturn 时，会有减少现金流的潮流；在行业 upturn 时，会有利用大量投资者资金的潮流。转向以人为中心的管理，部分是由美国员工的代际变化驱动的，千禧一代成为劳动力中更大的部分。从这些潮流中可以学到东西，但如果把它们当作绝对真理那就错了：迄今为止有效的东西不会在一夜之间变得无关紧要。

我的建议是，围绕某一组特定的基础信念来设计你的组织，并坚定地坚持这些信念至少三到四年，然后再考虑改变。改变可以是恢复性的，但如果你改变得太快，你就永远无法充分了解什么在起作用、什么不起作用，也就无法为未来的改变提供依据。
