运行你的工程入职项目

2023-03-06 · 阅读原文

大多数公司都表示，新入职的工程师需要三到六个月才能完全上手。工程领导者们心里清楚，如果承认自己团队的新工程师入职需要超过三到六个月，会显得不合时宜，所以他们嘴上这么说，但通常都认为新工程师要花更长时间才能完全发挥生产力。他们也知道，公司里最有影响力的工程师即便在公司工作多年后，生产力仍在持续提升。

运行工程入职项目，本质上是在优化两个紧密相关的问题：

  1. How do we increase the percentage of engineers who are reasonably productive at three months?
  2. How do we set the foundation that ends with more extremely impactful engineers a few years from now?

如果做得好，它能让新员工感到兴奋，并提升整体成功底线。事实上，在高速招聘的公司中，有效的入职培训，但不知为何，它常常被当作次要事务而被忽视。幸运的是，和许多被遗忘的流程一样，你可以在短时间内从几乎没有入职流程提升到相当不错的水平。超高速增长时代的生产力

为实现这一点，我们将逐一介绍：

  • Fundamental components of onboarding, including examples of several real companies onboarding processes
  • Role of executive sponsor, orchestrator, manager and buddy in a typical onboarding process
  • Curriculum to consider including in your onboarding
  • Why onboarding programs fail
  • Whether to integrate with wider company onboarding
  • When to prioritize onboarding

读完之后，你将获得一份逐步改进当前入职流程的计划，以及关于何时从临时性努力转向结构化入职项目的清晰视角。

这是 O'Reilly 出版的《The Engineering Executive’s Primer》一书中未经编辑的章节。

入职基础与示例

一个结构化的入职流程，归根结底是要配备若干关键角色（尤其是作为执行赞助人的你，以及负责协调入职的人），并确定入职项目中要包含的具体课程内容。

具体形式在不同公司会有所不同，因此我认为从几个真实世界的工程入职案例入手会很有帮助。在讨论这些细节时需要说明两点：第一，这些案例通常会省略公司在全公司层面为新员工提供的入职内容，以聚焦于工程团队自身的入职；第二，每家公司的入职流程都会随着时间演进，任何静态描述都只能捕捉某一特定阶段，以下提到的公司后来都对自己的入职流程进行了进一步的优化。

工程入职项目示例：

  • Digg: I showed up, got a company branded t-shirt and a laptop. My manager introduced me to the two other members of my team. They tried to issue me an integration environment, but the one engineer who knew how that worked had recently quit, so they told me to take over another recently departed engineer’s environment instead. With that, the onboarding was complete.It’s a bit messy, but a surprisingly large number of startups have effectively no onboarding beyond showing up, getting a laptop, and getting pointed to team mates who can help
  • Facebook: Facebook runs a six week Engineering Bootcamp, attended by every new engineering and engineering management hire, with a focus on facilitating team selection between new hires and Facebook’s teams, and teaching new hires Facebook’s approach to development. They work directly on Facebook’s software, often fixing bugs, and are supported by dedicated Facebook engineers who review their pull requests, run office hours, and so on.Facebook’s Bootcamp is the most frequently mentioned model of high-investment engineering onboarding. When considering copying components, keep in mind both the volume of engineers going through Bootcamp (10s and later 100s of concurrent new engineers), and that it solves for both onboarding and team matching, which is atypical of most companies’ hiring processes
  • Stripe: the first day focused on laptop setup, security practices, and getting familiar with Stripe’s development tools by making a small commit to the product website (eventually adding oneself to the company website’s About page). New engineering hires were then run through a curriculum ranging from architecture to observability.The final step of engineering onboarding group new hires into teams of four to six engineers. Each team worked on a project together, with one existing Stripe engineer serving as the project’s tech lead. These projects generally lasted 1-4 weeks, and were completed before releasing the teams to join their long-term teams

角色分工

人们很容易把大量精力投入到一次性设计入职流程上，但入职项目是一个活的系统，需要持续的维护。更好的起点是先思考运行这个流程所需的关键角色，特别是执行赞助人、项目协调人、团队经理和入职伙伴。

执行赞助人

作为工程高管，你很可能不会亲自运行工程入职项目。你可能会为每一批新员工主持一次入职分享，让他们知道你是一个真实存在的人，并建立联系，以便他们后续有顾虑时可以来找你。但你确实在入职工作中扮演着一个非常重要的角色——即担任入职项目的执行赞助人。

作为入职项目的执行赞助人，你应该：

  • Select the program orchestrator to operate and evolve the program
  • Align with the orchestrator on how to monitor the program over time (certainly through surveys, but ideally some objective data too)
  • Monitor the program data on a monthly basis
  • Meet directly with new hires who have exceptionally good or bad onboarding experiences to understand where things might be falling through the cracks
  • Celebrate the program on an ongoing basis so that people continue to prioritize its success, including supporting onboarding work as a valid contribution towards being promoted

着重说最后一点：即使公司在理性上知道入职很重要，也很难真正把它排到优先级。问题很容易被忽略，因为新员工不太可能刚到一家新公司就开始抱怨入职流程。

只有你，作为工程入职的执行赞助人，才拥有足够的高度和权限来长期维持一个有影响力的入职项目的质量标准。

项目协调人

如果说执行赞助人提供的是运行入职项目的组织资源，那么项目协调人就是把这些资源转化为一个活的项目的人。通常这个角色由一位工程经理兼任，同时承担其他职责，但在规模较大的公司，可能由来担任。谁来负责工程流程？

一般职责包括：

  • Develop and maintain the program’s curriculum: what content to prioritize? How many courses should you have? And so on
  • Evolve the program incrementally over time based on participant feedback and outcomes
  • Coordinate each onboarding class to ensure it runs effectively
  • Create onboarding templates that fit the curriculum, and are used by each new hire’s manager to continue their onboarding beyond the program itself
  • Align with the executive sponsor on monitoring the program’s outcomes. This should typically include a post onboarding survey to all participants, a summary of survey responses, and data trends over time. You may also want to look at 衡量工程组织 to understand where to focus subsequent onboarding investments
  • Address the little mishaps that naturally happen in a program like this, e.g. a segment’s teacher calling out sick for the day

选择一位有效的项目协调人是打造成功入职项目的头等大事，而且你需要反复做这个决定，因为人会流动。根据我的经验，最重要的考量因素是：

  • Real experience operating as an engineer at your company (or relationships to engage those with that experience)
  • Energized by people as individuals rather than compliance to a process
  • Ability to use feedback to drive ongoing improvement

在公司不是特别大（每月新入职工程师不到 20 人）的时候，入职协调人通常是兼职角色，但这绝不是一件轻松的任务。负责这个项目的人每月需要投入 20 小时以上。一位工程经理可以同时带团队和负责此事，但这样他就几乎没有时间做其他事情了。

团队经理

即使最好的入职流程也会有缺口，而且会优先考虑常规情况而非个体差异，这时候新员工的直接经理就变得至关重要。入职项目关注的是帮助新员工有一个好的开始，而经理最终要对新员工在公司的长期成功负责。

一般职责包括：

  • Create a personalized onboarding document for each new hire, starting from a template created by the program orchestrator
  • Start weekly 1:1s with the new hire. Even if you prefer less frequent 1:1s, it’s very valuable to have them weekly for a new hire’s first several months
  • Assign an onboarding buddy to the new hire. In some cases, this is handled by by the program orchestrator, in which case you should ensure an appropriate buddy is assigned
  • Partner with the onboarding buddy to identify a new hire’s first several projects. Finding safe learning projects that also provide real value to the business is an art, and will significantly accelerate the new hire’s impact and sense of belonging
  • Make sure the new hire has actually been added to the various team meetings, chat rooms, email groups, and so on. Even if it’s supposed to happen automatically, this stuff gets routinely messed up, and can be very demoralizing for new hires when it happens

值得承认的是，经理们自己也面临各种压力，比如要交付一个延期的项目，或者要补充一个小型生产 on-call 轮班。让人惊讶的是，经理的短期目标和入职项目的长期目标之间常常会出现摩擦，例如经理会要求把新员工从入职培训中拉出来，去赶一个关键 deadline。项目协调人应该通过不断提醒经理入职对团队成功的重要性，来对抗这种倾向。

入职伙伴

最后一个重要角色是入职伙伴。具体形式因公司而异，但在大多数情况下，他们是新员工在团队内的第二个联系人。伙伴可以回答关于开发、环境搭建和初期项目的具体问题。当团队经理无法及时回复时，他们也是一个很好的后备选项。

一般职责包括：

  • Bring the new hire along with you to meetings, to grab lunch, and to meet other members of the company. Don’t let them linger alone! This is especially true in remote companies, where a bit of effort will transform the company from an anonymous wall of faces into a community
  • Schedule daily time with the new hire for their first several weeks. This can be 15 or 30 minutes with some time blocked afterwards in case it runs long. The goal is to resolve any blocking issues or topics for the new hire. Having something scheduled helps minimize the common scenario where the new hire feels awkward asking for help directly
  • After one to two weeks, chat with the new hire about whether it’s helpful to continue the meetings for another month or two. This will come down to their personality and preferences more than any sort of best practice
  • Keep in touch with the new hire’s manager about areas where things are going well or where they’re running into problems. It can be tempting to ignore early problems, but this is really the only chance you’ll have to address behavioral and cultural misalignment before they calcify. Feedback may not always be a gift, but it’s particularly valuable early

课程内容

一旦你确定了入职项目的协调人，接下来就要决定课程内容——到底应该重点教什么？作为一个可以开始实验的起点，我建议设置三个模块：

  • Engineering values and strategy
  • Technical architecture
  • Spinning up the development environment

在有了这三个概览课程之后，可以从两个角度继续完善：

  • Survey new hires a month after onboarding to ask what they wish had been covered
  • Survey manager and tech leads about areas where new hires are struggling. These might be technical, but is even more likely to be cultural

这样做能引导你走上一条有价值的路径，避免陷入无穷无尽的可能主题中。特别是，它能帮助你避开那些理论上有用但没人真正感兴趣的内容。

另一个需要考虑的问题是，是否在入职项目中加入实际项目环节，比如 Facebook Bootcamp 的编程部分，或 Stripe 的入职项目小组。一般来说，如果你投入大量精力去做，这些项目效果很好，否则往往不太理想。需要记住，不做入职驱动的项目，替代方案是各团队自己为新员工挑选项目，这种方式通常效果不错，也不需要中央协调。

最后，入职课程中一个隐含但很重要的部分，是让同期入职的人互相认识。当你只看学到的信息和结果指标时，很容易忽略新员工自己经常提到的入职最大价值：认识其他同期新员工，建立一个可以问问题、一起吃午饭的人际网络。

谁可以参加工程入职？

在任何入职项目运行过程中，都会周期性地出现“谁必须参加、谁可以参加”的问题。我的建议是要求所有工程师和所有工程经理都参加，不论级别。有些非常资深的工程师和经理会试图跳过，但我认为不应该允许。入职不仅教“事情是怎么做的”，更重要的是“为什么这么做”。对高级别新员工来说，最大的风险就是他们在还没有理解“为什么”之前，就试图改变“怎么做”。特别是那些坚持说自己不需要参加的高级新员工，更应该强制要求他们参加。

如果其他职能部门（例如产品）提出想要参加工程入职，我的建议是最初不要主动安排他们，但允许他们个别申请加入后续的入职批次。这样既能让真正感兴趣的人参与，又不必把工程入职改造成对没有工程背景的人也有吸引力的形式。

入职项目为什么会失败

人们很容易认为入职项目失败是因为没人足够努力去创建一个好项目。当然，有些公司确实没有在入职上投入，这是错失的机会，但那并不是导致失败的主因。大多数项目失败，是因为有人——带着非常好的意图——创建了一个过于复杂、过于繁重的项目。

典型的演变过程是这样的：

  • A new program orchestrator is selected to run engineering onboarding
  • They develop a new, more involved program that addresses all the identified needs, although it happens to take a lot of energy to operate
  • The new program launches, and everyone loves it. Finally, onboarding is solved!
  • Over time, it’s hard to get enough internal engagement to run the program. People still love the program, but have too many conflicting priorities
  • The orchestrator gets frustrated and transitions to another role. Te program is run by their replacement, who often doesn’t have the original orchestrator’s passion for the area
  • The program becomes stale and bureaucratic
  • The program is scaled back significantly to something much simpler

当你考虑修改入职流程时，一定要确保它是协调人能够长期维护的。它必须是在新项目刚上线时的兴奋感消退、工程经理不再给协调人发“感谢”卡之后，你仍然能持续运行的东西。最糟糕的入职项目，往往是努力过头，而不是努力不够。

与公司整体入职整合

除了工程入职，你的公司很可能还有一个覆盖全公司的入职项目。该项目可能会涉及工程入职中很多重要的主题，特别是用户、年度目标或公司价值观等。你应该充分借助这些公司级入职的内容，避免重复制作。

更具争议的问题往往是：负责公司整体入职的团队是否也应该负责工程入职。例如，如果是 People Programs 团队在运行入职，他们是否也应该来安排工程团队的入职课程？

我的经验是，通常应该继续由工程团队内部来运行入职。原因有几个：

  • There is usually significant support for handing off the program to a centralized function to run it, because the program orchestrator sees it as an opportunity to take on a new role.However, the people who replace the program orchestrator will be less focused on effective engineering onboarding, and will not have experience operating as an engineer at your company. They will approach engineering onboarding with a focus on making it more consistent with the shared company onboarding process, even if that means eliminating key aspects of what makes engineering onboarding effective. This isn’t because they’re bad people, it’s just the natural incentives of a centralized function
  • Once another function is responsible for running your onboarding process, experimenting with improvements will become much more difficult, as it requires cross-functional coordination with a team who are often very busy. Increasing the cost of experimentation will significantly harm onboarding quality over time
  • Feedback and onboarding quality data will often become sensitive data within the other function, only infrequently shared with you after being sanitized. Rather than driving your onboarding with data, including knowing who to reach out to about problems, you may lose access to that data

有时候实际情况会让继续掌控工程入职流程变得过于痛苦，那也可以接受。有时候这些问题的所有权会变得非常政治化，难以继续保有；高管领导力的关键是选择当下最好的路径，而不是在每一把剑上都死磕。

何时应优先考虑入职

当你每个月都在稳定招聘一定数量的新员工时，入职项目的影响力尤其大。如果你每月有十名新员工，那么以班级形式进行培训在经济性上就会开始超过一对一培训。当你开始加速招聘时这一点会更明显，因为你现有的团队既要忙于招聘，又会逐渐对入职新工程师感到生疏。

即使招聘规模不大，好的入职仍然是一项很有价值的投入。有效地帮助新员工快速上手总是值得的。我唯一不建议投入大量精力做入职的情况是，你招聘频率极低，以至于入职材料在第二次使用之前就已经过时。在这种情况下，更好的做法是对每位新员工进行个性化入职，而不是设计一个大规模的项目。需要记住，这不仅和人员增长有关，也和你的自然流失率有关。很多公司虽然工程团队整体没有扩张，但由于流失，仍然在招聘大量新成员。

最后，记住要通过不断实验找到适合自己的入职强度。从一个几天就能拼凑起来的简单版本开始，或许这就已经足够了。
