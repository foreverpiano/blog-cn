选拔项目负责人

2018-07-12 · 阅读原文

你是否曾在一家公司工作过，总看到同样的两个人拿到最重要的项目？我也有过这种经历。从旁观者的角度看着这些学习和成长的机会溜走，确实令人沮丧。而且过度依赖一小群人，在公司规模扩大时很容易成为瓶颈。这一点非常重要，以至于我逐渐相信：拥有一大批能够主导关键项目的人，是衡量组织健康程度的最重要指标之一。

这是一个特别有力的指标，因为它同时衡量了公司执行项目的能力，以及其成员获得成长机会的程度。前者决定了公司的潜在产能，后者则与包容性高度相关。

在这个背景下，项目可以分为两类：关键项目和其他项目。关键项目是稀缺的，想做的人远多于能获得机会的人。而其他项目则相对充足——你可能无法立刻拿到，但如果等上一两个月，机会就会出现。对于这类充足的项目，没必要搞得太结构化！

为了让更多人有机会主导关键项目，我不断迭代，形成了一套结构化的流程，实际效果相当不错：

  1. Define the project’s scope and goals in a short document. Particularly important are identifying:time commitment: so folks can decide if they need to ask permission from their manager,requirements to apply: if there are no requirements, say so explicitly, a lot of folks will opt out, assuming there are),selection criteria: if multiple folks apply, how will you select the project leader between them?
  2. Announce the project to a public email list, at an all hands, over Slack, or however your company does persistent communication; I tend to use email for these. What’s most important is that you:allow folks to apply in private - some folks will be uncomfortable applying in public,that they don’t see who else has applied - some folks will see someone they view as senior apply, and will immediately disengage because they feel they are less qualified,whenever possible, give at least three working days for people to apply, as some folks will need to talk with their manager or peers to get confident in applying.
  3. Nudge folks to apply who you think would be good candidates but who might not self-select. Particularly important for getting new folks into the process.
  4. Select a project leader based on the selection criteria you specified. Take the time to consider every single applicant against the criteria, and if possible write up a paragraph or two about each of them. Once you’ve selected the leader, privately reach out to them to confirm that they’re able to commit.
  5. Sponsor the project leader by finding someone who has successfully completed a similar project to be their advisor. This sponsor will be accountable for coaching the leader to successful completion. This is a great learning opportunity for the sponsor, as they are typically folks who are great at doing things themselves, but not as used to teaching others how to lead large and ambiguous projects.
  6. Notify other folks who applied that they were not selected. It’s extremely helpful if you provide them feedback on why you didn’t select them. Sometimes it’s because they’ve already done something great and you want to create room for another person to learn, and that’s a totally reasonable thing to tell them!
  7. Kickoff the project, notifying the same folks you Announced to who the project leader is, who the sponsor is, and their plan for running the project.
  8. Record the project, who was selected, and who is the sponsor into a public spreadsheet. Also link out to a project brief!

长期坚持下来，你就会清楚地看到谁在被委以最重要的项目；如果做得好，你会发现这个群体在不断扩大。

刚开始这么做的时候，你会觉得非常束手束脚、效率低下。以前你可能只是私下 ping 一下信任的人，对方就立刻启动了；现在却要走一个更慢、更慎重的流程。但我越来越相信，这是过去几年来我在领导力方法上最重要的改变之一。如果做得好，它能成为你打造一家具有包容性组织的核心基石。

我曾在中简要提到过这个想法，但可能把最核心的内容埋得太深了，所以有了这篇帖子！让少数群体参与到一线工作中
