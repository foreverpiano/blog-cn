抬杠者、招聘委员会，以及其他提升招聘质量的复杂方法

2022-08-28 · 阅读原文

当 Uber Engineering 团队扩张到 800 名工程师时，工程部门被大致划分为五个工程总监负责。大部分工程和技术流程问题都在这五个组织内部局部解决。这种方式在多数情况下运行良好，但也导致核心流程缺乏一致性。招聘环节尤其碎片化，每个团队的经理都需要自行设计面试、问题和流程结构。

然而，无论最初是由哪个团队招聘的工程师，几乎所有团队最终都会与其合作。随着时间推移，一些团队对其他团队被认为过于宽松的招聘标准越来越不满。最终，三位总监对第四位总监的招聘标准极为不满，成功推动引入了借鉴 Amazon 的“bar raiser”（抬杠者）计划。在该计划中，每次招聘至少要有一位来自招聘部门之外、资质良好的面试官参与意见。理论上，抬杠者是是否发出 offer 的最终决定者，确保每位被录用者至少符合两个组织的招聘标准。作为最初推行抬杠者计划的成员，我可以证明它解决了许多问题，尽管它难以应对那些对任何与自己意见相左的抬杠者决定都要向上级申诉的招聘经理。

后来我加入 Stripe 时，已经深刻吸取了关于抬杠者计划的核心教训：如果缺乏大量持续的、有权力的组织支持，这类计划很容易被悄无声息地规避。因此我反对引入抬杠者，并乐于支持建立招聘委员会。结果你猜怎么着，招聘委员会也带来了它自身的一些挑战。

接下来我们将讨论：是否应该引入改进招聘质量的计划、如何运行抬杠者计划、如何运作招聘委员会，以及我最推荐的结构化审批方法。

你是否应该优先考虑招聘质量？

运营一个成功的工程组织，很大一部分在于尽可能避免不必要的流程，但也不能完全没有。在考虑是否需要提升招聘质量时，以下三个问题很有帮助：

  1. Have you already created a 招聘写作索引 and trained your team on using it? If not, go do that first.
  2. Do you have a general concern about hiring quality, or is it a narrow problem related to a small number of individuals? If it’s just a few folks, introduce a 命名、层级与校准。, and use it to manage those individuals up or out.
  3. Are you still concerned about the quality of your hiring after ramping up on structured hiring and performance management systems?? Alright, let’s introduce a program to improve hiring quality!

通常而言，这些流程在快速扩张招聘期间最有价值，尤其是在许多招聘经理都是公司新人的时候。如果你认为在招聘节奏缓慢或招聘经理任期很长的情况下仍有必要引入这些做法，我的第一反应是你的团队存在绩效问题，需要直接面对，而引入新流程只是推迟处理这个问题的表现。

抬杠者（Bar Raisers）

自从 Amazon 推出抬杠者计划以来，许多公司对其进行了调整和复制。正如开篇所述，Uber 也在 2015 年左右采用了抬杠者计划，其运作方式如下：

  1. Interview loops were generally designed by each teams’ engineering manager
  2. However, all loops must include a bar raiser as an interviewer. The bar raiser must come from a different Director’s reporting chain than the loop’s hiring engineering manager
  3. Bar raisers gave an interview of their choice, rather than an interview assigned by the loop’s hiring engineering manager
  4. Bar raisers were selected for strong interviewing skills, evidence of experience interviewing at Uber specifically, and were approved by the team leading the bar raiser program. Bar raisers went through a brief training program before starting bar raiser interviews

引入抬杠者是对面试流程本身的一个简单修改，却显著改变了 Uber 整体的招聘结果。最大的运营挑战有时是隐形的，核心在于如何解决抬杠者、招聘经理及其汇报链之间的冲突。如果没有高管层面的支持来维护抬杠者的决定，这个角色的影响力很容易被招聘经理汇报链频繁推翻而被削弱。

优点

  • Decentralized, scales effectively as number of interviews scales
  • Avoids slowing down process by introducing an additional approval step
  • Multiple potential bar raisers for any given interview loop mean that hiring quality remains relatively high even if a percentage of bar raisers are poorly calibrated

缺点

  • Susceptible to overriding escalations from hiring management chain, undermining impact and morale of bar raisers
  • Unclear incentives for the bar raisers themselves, given the work is often not valued in promotion process
  • Decentralized day-to-day operation can create ambiguous role for executive sponsor, which often translates into disengaged rather than accountable sponsorship

检验问题

  • What are your offer-extend and offer-accept rates?
  • How often do bar raisers change the outcome of an interview?
  • How many bar raisers do you have?
  • How many bar raisers have you added/lost in the past quarter?
  • What is the interview load on bar raisers?
  • How does the executive sponsor know if structure needs to be adjusted?

招聘委员会

直到 2016 年，Stripe 的工程招聘采用的是“池化招聘”（pool hiring）模式：候选人进入流程后，会由来自整个组织的工程师和一位经理进行面试，随后该经理和招聘人员会在面试汇报会（debrief，因历史原因内部称为“trope”）中综合反馈并做出录用决定。所谓“池化招聘”，是指参与招聘的面试官（包括经理）通常不来自候选人最终可能加入的团队。

随着我们开始招聘更多高级别候选人，我们逐渐放弃了池化招聘，因为高级候选人更希望了解他们将加入的团队。作为经理，去推销一个自己也只是模糊了解的团队非常困难；候选人往往最终想加入面试经理所在的团队，而不是公司最需要补充人力的地方。

从池化招聘转向团队特定招聘也带来了一些挑战，特别是招聘流程一致性的丧失。当同一个团队和同一名经理负责所有职位面试时，你会发现各团队的招聘流程开始分化。为了解决这种分化，我们在 2018 年左右（当时公司约有 800 名工程师）引入了招聘委员会。

招聘委员会流程的一种版本运作如下（我说“一种版本”，是因为细节随时间有不少变化，但核心部分相对稳定）：

  1. Candidates interview for specific teams’ roles, and are interviewed by the team and hiring manager they would work with
  2. Hiring Manager makes a tentative hiring decision and writes up a hiring proposal for Hiring Committee
  3. Hiring Committee would review hiring proposals in a standing 30 minute meeting every other day and approve/reject/send them back to the hiring manager for more detail
  4. Recruiter and Hiring Manager would coordinate follow up steps (either pitching the candidate, sending them back to Hiring Committee, or informing them they weren’t moving forward)

这个流程对我们来说运行良好，尽管有些经理不满它让招聘过程多花了一到两天（如果招聘经理准备提案较慢，有时会更久）。它也偶尔受到判断不准的委员会成员的影响，需要持续关注并加以解决。

优点

  • Higher consistency of hiring decisions due to smaller number of committee members
  • Hiring committee members learn from and calibrate with each other quickly
  • Generates a centralized, clean data source to improve organizational hiring

缺点

  • Introduces, at minimum, an additional 1-2 day delay in hiring process
  • Significant time load on committee members, which may not be valued as high impact work
  • Hiring outcomes are disproportionately impacted with only a few miscalibrated committee members

检验问题

  • What are your offer-extend and offer-accept rates?
  • How quickly are hiring proposals approved/rejected?
  • What percentage of hiring proposals are approved, rejected, or returned for additional information?
  • What is the tenure of managers writing packets that are rejected or returned for additional information?
  • Are there managers whose proposals are always approved? Can you streamline the process to accelerate that?
  • Are there managers whose proposals are rarely approved? Can you provide improved education and training for those managers?
  • How does the executive sponsor know if structure needs to be adjusted?

结构化审批

人们很容易认为每个招聘流程都应该已经为每个步骤明确了责任人，但我的实际经验是，许多招聘流程是模糊、不确定且不清晰的——解决这个问题比推出招聘委员会或抬杠者计划要容易得多。我见过结构良好、责任清晰的招聘流程取得了极大成功，我将其称为“结构化审批”。

我们在 Calm 工程团队通常这样运行这个流程：

  1. Recruiter is responsible for sourcing, deciding on advancing forward to onsite, scheduling interviews
  2. Hiring Manager makes initial decision to make candidate an offer after gathering feedback on candidate performance, and hosting a hiring debrief
  3. Recruiter creates a private Slack channel including Hiring Manager, Recruiting Manager, Head of Engineering, and managers (if any) in reporting chain between Hiring Manager and Head of Engineering
  4. Hiring Manager writes a 1-page offer rationale, and shares it in the private Slack channel
  5. Recruiter makes initial compensation proposal based on compensation bands, and shares in Slack channel
  6. Head of Engineering has 24 working hours to review and approve the offer and the compensation proposal. If they miss the window, the offer and proposal are approved by default. They may send either component back for a second iteration to collect more feedback or modify the offer. Approval and coordination is done within Slack channel
  7. Recruiter extends offer, coordinates closes, and archives Slack channel on final outcome
  8. Head of Engineering meets with head of engineering recruiting on weekly basis to debug hiring process and work through any specific issues that have come up

在每个阶段我们都有明确的责任人，Slack 频道为参与特定候选人招聘的人员提供了可见性，我们还频繁开会来调试和改进基础的结构化流程。这并不是说这个具体流程适合所有人，它会根据你的团队、招聘节奏等因素而有所不同。但我确信一定存在一个适合你的版本，即使在较大规模下依然有效（例如，很容易想象在某个节点之后，只有 Staff-plus 及 Director-plus 级别的 offer 需要升级到工程负责人审批，其他 offer可以由更接近招聘团队的层级做出）。

优点

  • Low coordination cost
  • Avoids diffuse accountability across many parties
  • Role of executive sponsor is clear, unambiguous, and fails loudly

缺点

  • No inherent quality control mechanics, outcomes are wholly dependent on individuals involved
  • Many specific structures are slow, often introducing 1+ day delay in hiring
  • Requires care to avoid degrading into 遵循政策，而非例外。

检验问题

  • What are your offer-extend and offer-accept rates?
  • How long does it take new managers and recruiters to ramp up on process?
  • How long does approval take? Drilling in, what parts of approval are slow? Are they slow for everyone, or just folks ramping up?
  • How many rounds of back-and-forth does it take to align on a compensation proposal?
  • How does the executive sponsor know if structure needs to be adjusted?

推荐建议

在经历过使用所有这些机制的公司后，我对如何提升组织招聘质量的默认建议是：

  • Introduced structured approval when you introduce your second hiring manager in a function (e.g. second engineering manager)
  • Wait until organization trust grows sufficiently weak that there’s significant skepticism about hiring quality across teams, then introduce a hiring committee
  • Avoid introducing bar raising unless you have a clear thesis on why the other approaches won’t work out

与所有组织流程一样，好的流程是演化出来的，而非设计出来的。（好的流程是演化而来的，而非设计出来的）：在推行时要深思熟虑，并在它从概念转变为实践的过程中持续关注。

你可能注意到我没有在任何场景下推荐抬杠者计划。确实，我真的没有发现它特别有效，但我并不是说它对某些公司无效，只是我见过三个地方尝试采用它，却从未见过它在实践中运行良好，因此我不推荐。相反，我发现招聘委员会通常运行得相当不错，只有在缺乏维护的情况下才会失效，所以如果你有积极投入的高管支持者，我是推荐的。

就像所有事情一样，实际效果可能因人而异。
