如何采用大语言模型（LLMs）？

2024-05-14 · 阅读原文

无论你是产品工程师、产品经理，还是工程高管，你很可能都被推动去考虑使用大型语言模型（Large Language Models，简称 LLMs）来扩展你的产品或优化你的流程。2023-2024 年是 LLM 采用的一个有趣时代，这些能力已进入主流，许多公司担心自己正在落后，尽管大多数集成看起来仍停留在表面。

这一背景让 LLM 采用成为战略案例研究的绝佳主题。本文档是一份工程战略文档，探讨了一家假设的公司——Theoretical Ride Sharing——如何采用 LLMs。

在深入战略之前，先补充一下场景：Theoretical 拥有 2000 名员工，其中 300 人为软件工程师。公司已融资 4 亿美元，年收入 5000 万美元，目前在北美和欧洲的 200 个城市运营。他们是一家网约车公司，类似于 Uber 或 Lyft。不过，他们的创新在于使用更大的车辆——本质上是在重塑公共交通。

这是《Crafting Engineering Strategy》中的一章。

政策

Theoretical Ride Sharing 使用 LLMs 的综合政策如下：

  • Develop an LLM-backed process for reactivating departed and suspended drivers in mature markets. Through modeling 建模司机入职流程, we determined that improving onboarding time will have little impact on the total number of active drivers. Instead, we are focusing on mechanisms to reactivate departed and suspended drivers, which is the only opportunity to meaningfully impact active drivers.Report on progress monthly in Exec Weekly Meeting, coordinated in #exec-weekly
  • Start with Anthropic. We use Anthropic models, which are available through our existing cloud provider via AWS Bedrock. To avoid maintaining multiple implementations, where we view the underlying foundational model quality to be somewhat undifferentiated, we are not looking to adopt a broad set of LLMs at this point. This is anchored in our Wardley 地图分析 LLM 生态系统.Exceptions will be reviewed by the Machine Learning Review in #ml-review
  • Developer experience team (DX) must offer at least one LLM-backed developer productivity tool. This tool should enhance the experience, speed, or quality of writing software in TypeScript. This tool should help us develop our thinking for next year, such that we have conviction increasing (or decreasing!) our investment. This tool should be available to all engineers. Adopting one tool is the required baseline, if DX identifies further interesting tools, e.g. Github Copilot, they are empowered to bring the request to the Engineering Exec team for review. Review will focus on balancing our rate of learning, vendor cost, and data security. We’ve 建模 LLM 对开发者体验的影响.Vendor approvals to be reviewed in #cto
  • Internal Tools team (INT) must offer at least one LLM-backed ad-hoc prompting tool. This tool should support arbitrary non-engineering use cases for LLMs, such as text extraction, rewriting notes, and so on. It must be usable with customer data while also honoring our existing data processing commitments. This tool should be available to all employees.Vendor approvals to be reviewed in #coo
  • Refresh policy in six months. Our primary goal is to quickly learn about this unfamiliar domain where we have limited internal expertise, then review whether we should increase our investment afterwards.Flag questions and suggestions in #cto

诊断

以下是 Theoretical Ride Sharing 在采用 LLMs 时面临的主要挑战总结：

  1. There are, at minimum, three distinct needs that folks internally are asking us to solve (either separately or with a shared solution):productivity tools for non-engineers, e.g. ad-hoc document rewriting, document summarizationproductivity tools for engineers, e.g. advanced autocomplete tooling like Github Copilotproduct extensions, e.g. high-quality document extraction in driver onboarding workflows
  2. Of the above, we see product extensions are potential strategic differentiation, and the other two as workflow optimizations that improve our productivity but don’t necessarily differentiate us from the broader industry. Some of the opportunities for strategic differentiation we see are:Reactivating the departed and suspended drivers is our largest lever to increasing active drivers, as explored in our 建模司机入职流程Faster driver onboarding with less human involvement will not increase active drivers, but we see a clear opportunity for LLMs to reduce operating costs, which may be worthwhile even if it doesn’t address the core problem of active driversImproved customer support by increasing the response speed and quality of our responses to customer inquiries
  3. We currently have limited experience or expertise in using LLMs in the company and in the industry. Prolific thought leadership to the contrary, there are very few companies or products using LLMs in scaled, differentiated ways. That’s currently true for us as well
  4. We want to develop our expertise without making an irreversible commitment. We think that our internal expertise is a limiter for effective problem selection and utilization of LLMs, and that developing our expertise will help us become more effective in iterative future decisions on this topic. Conversely, we believe that making a major investment now, prior to developing our in-house expertise, would be relatively high risk and low reward given no other industry players appear to have identified a meaningful advantage at this point
  5. Switching across foundational models and foundational model providers is cheap. This is true both economically (low financial commitment) and from an integration cost perspective (APIs and usage is largely consistent across providers)
  6. Foundational models and providers are evolving rapidly, and it’s unclear how the space will evolve. It’s likely that current foundational model providers will train one or two additional generations of foundational models with larger datasets, but at some point they will become cost prohibitive to train (e.g. the next major version of OpenAI or Anthropic models seem likely to cost $500m+ to train). Differentiation might move into developer-experience at that point. Open source models like LLaMa might become significantly cost-advantaged. Or something else entirely. The future is wide open.We’ve built a Wardley map to understand the Wardley 地图分析 LLM 生态系统.
  7. Training a foundational model is prohibitively expensive for our needs. We’ve raised $400m, and training a competitive foundational model would cost somewhere between $3m to $100m to match the general models provided by Anthropic or OpenAI

探索

大语言模型（LLMs）构建在基础模型之上。训练这些基础模型极为昂贵，而且随着对更复杂模型的竞争加剧，成本还在不断上升。据称 Meta 训练 LLaMa 2 花费了 2000-3000 万美元，而 LLaMa 1 的训练成本约为 300 万美元。OpenAI 的 GPT-4 据称训练成本达 1 亿美元。在语料质量及其与任务相关性存在一定差异的前提下，较大模型通常优于较小模型，因此除非你拥有大量独特的数据集，否则没有太多动力去训练更小的基础模型。即使有独特数据集，你也可能更适合做微调（fine-tuning）或上下文学习（In-Context Learning，简称 ICL）。

Anthropic 对每百万输入 token 的收费在 0.25 到 15 美元之间，输出 token 略高一些。OpenAI 的收费则在 0.50 到 60 美元之间，输出同样略高。英文平均每个单词约 1.3 个 token，这意味着你可以完成大量 LLM 工作，而花费远低于大多数风投支持的初创公司在零食上的开支。

关于 LLMs 是否已达到性能提升将放缓的阶段，目前存在大量争议。这很像围绕摩尔定律是否已死亡的持久争论，我们不清楚未来 LLM 性能还能提升多少。从训练成本角度看，企业不太可能仅靠增加算力投入就能持续改进基础模型。很少有公司能承受 10 亿美元的训练成本，能承受 100 亿美元的就更少了，很难想象会有公司在建造价值 1000 亿美元的模型。不过，算法改进和对数据集的投资很可能在不显著增加算力成本的情况下推动性能提升。在这个领域唯一能高度确定的预测是：未来 3 年内，模型性能很可能还会再翻一到两次，之后可能继续按此速度提升，也可能在这个性能水平上趋于平稳——两种结果都有可能。

对于某些决策，必须从一开始就做对。例如，从 AWS 迁移到 Azure 成本极高，因为定制化和锁定程度很高。然而，LLMs 似乎不属于这一类别。与行业同行交流后发现，大多数公司都在同时试验来自 Anthropic、OpenAI 以及其他厂商（例如 Mistral）的多种模型。不同模型的行为确实存在差异，但现有模型的行为也会随时间变化（例如据称 GPT-3.5 变得越来越“懒”），这意味着即使只采用一种模型，处理模型差异的开销也难以避免。从技术角度看，模型的供应商锁定程度较低。不过，监管要求——如更新数据处理协议（Data Processing Agreements）——在切换供应商时会带来一定摩擦。

尽管人工智能领域正经历投资热潮，但大多数规模化的科技公司仍在寻找如何将这些能力用于超越 GitHub Copilot 这类明显且普遍实践的用途。例如，Stripe 正在大力投资 LLMs 以提升内部生产力，其中很可能包括让模型完成此前由员工负责的某些内部任务，比如验证公司网站是否与其在入驻申请中提供的信息一致。但目前还不清楚他们是否已经找到一种能显著改变其产品或用户体验的方法。

更具体地看网约车公司，目前似乎也没有出现突破性的行业特定做法。Uber 同样在采用 LLMs 来提升内部生产力，并取得了一些运营效率改进，这些在其 2023 年 8 月发布的关于使用 LLMs 进行内部开发者和运营生产力投入的文章，以及 2024 年 5 月更详细介绍这些努力的文章中均有记录。
