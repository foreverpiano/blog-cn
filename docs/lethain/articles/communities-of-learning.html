学习社区

2018-09-01 · 阅读原文

我一直更喜欢独自学习。如果遇到难题，我只要一个人安静几个小时，通常就能把它搞清楚。但如果要我在别人注视下当场解决，我甚至不知道该从哪里开始。这部分是因为内向性格，但更主要的是，我非常不舒服在公开场合犯错。像很多人一样，我的大脑仍会好心地提醒我几十年前在公众面前犯过的错误，而那些记忆至今仍让我感到困扰。

很长一段时间里，这种不适让我错过了在支持性工作环境中最有价值的一件事：和同事一起建立学习社区。这种做法在一个高度默契的 把同侪当作你的第一团队。 中效果尤其好。最近，我花了更多时间去推动一个更广泛的工程经理学习社区。

刚开始组织这个小组时，我们把重点放在内容详实的演示上。每张幻灯片都塞满了重要的经验和关键细节。但效果并不好。大家参与度不高，出席率也逐渐下降，学习并没有成为当天的主旋律。

从那以后，我们不断迭代形式，最终意外发现了一种持续有效的做法：

  1. Be a facilitator, not a lecturer. Folks want to learn from each other more than they want to learn from a single presenter. Step back and facilitate.
  2. Brief presentations, long discussions. Present a few minutes of content, maybe five, and then move into discussion. Keep the discussions short enough that even if a group doesn’t get traction on a given topic, it don’t become awkward. Ten minutes is a good cap.
  3. Small breakout groups. Giving folks time to discuss in small groups allows them to learn a bit about the topic in a small, safe place. It also gives everyone an opportunity to be part of the discussion, which is a lot more engaging than listening to others for an hour.
  4. Bring learnings to full group. After discussions, give each group an opportunity to bring their discussion back to the larger group, to allow the groups to cross-pollinate their learnings.
  5. Topics that people already know about. Successful topics are ones that folks have already thought about, typically because they are core to their daily work. Ideally it’s something that they do but would like to get better at, such as 1:1s, mentorship, coaching, or career development.People find it very hard to discuss content they’ve just learned if it is too far away from their previous experience. It also creates an environment where learning has to come from the facilitator as opposed to from the group at large.
  6. Encourage tenured folks to attend. For many learning communities, you’ll find that the most senior or most tenured folks opt out to focus on other work. This is a shame, because there is so much for them to teach newer folks, and also because it creates an opportunity for them to learn from and get to know new members.
  7. Optional pre-reads. Some folks aren’t comfortable being introduced to a new topic in public, and for those folks providing a list of optional pre-reads can help them prepare for the discussion. I find that most folks don’t read them (which, surprisingly, I also found true when hosting 主持技术论文阅读小组), but for some folks they’re very helpful.
  8. Checking in. Depending on the size of the group, it can be powerful to start by checking in with each other, giving a twenty or thirty second self-introduction from each person. The format we’ve been using lately is your name, your team and one sentence on what’s on your mind. This is especially useful in quickly growing communities, as it makes it easier for folks to meet each other.

我最喜欢这种形式的地方在于，它真正给了大家想要的东西——花时间互相学习，而且作为 facilitator 准备起来也非常轻松。我远不是一个经验丰富的 facilitator，但我也发现这种形式为我自己提供了一个有收获且安全的成长机会。

如果你的公司还没有任何学习社区，不妨尝试一下。我发现这是我有机会参与的最简单、也最有价值的事情之一。
