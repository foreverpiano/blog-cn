成为工程高管

2023-01-08 · 阅读原文

我25岁时开始了第一次高管职位求职。 最终我收到了一家只有四名工程师的初创公司发来的工程负责人offer，但我拒绝了它，选择加入Uber。 直到十年后，我才加入Calm，开启了我的第一个高管职位。 如果你开始研究高管职业路径，你会发现有些人名义上21岁就通过创办公司成为工程高管， 也有些人在职业生涯30多年后仍未担任过工程高管。

正如这些轶事所表明的，获得工程高管职位并没有“唯一途径”。 然而，你听到的关于寻找高管职位的故事越多，它们听起来就越相似。 如果你正寻求高管职位，我把多年来听到的众多故事，以及我自己的经历，提炼成一套可重复的流程——这是潜在候选人通常会遵循的路径。

明确来说，我在这里将“工程高管”定义为“在一家公司负责整个工程职能的领导角色”。 这个定义与具体头衔无关，因为不同公司使用头衔的方式并不一致。 有时公司会把CTO、工程副总裁或工程负责人等头衔授予只管理部分工程团队的人。 另一些时候，你会看到工程副总裁向CTO汇报，但实际由副总裁承担所有职能领导职责。 你还会发现有些公司把所有200名工程总监都称为高管。 这都没问题，只是本文中“工程高管”一词的含义并非如此！

这是O’Reilly出版的《The Engineering Executive’s Primer》一书中未经编辑的章节。

为什么要追求高管职位？

如果你刚开始第一次高管职位求职，你应该对一个会被多次问到的问题有清晰的答案： “你为什么要找高管职位？” 为自己想清楚这个答案很重要，因为它将在整个求职过程中成为宝贵的指引。 如果你不确定答案，花时间认真思考，直到得出清晰的结论（或许可以结合职业体检模板来思考）。

一旦你把理由写下来，和几位已经担任过高管职位的同行或导师一起审视。 吸收他们的反馈，然后就完成了。

另一方面，面试官也非常好奇你追求高管职位的原因，但未必是你以为的那个原因。 他们并非只是在寻找你独特的故事（当然，一段令人难忘的独特故事他们肯定喜欢）， 而是试图过滤掉带有危险信号的候选人：自负、嫉妒、过度的地位导向，以及三心二意。

所有高管职位和流程都是“one of one”。

对于非高管职位，良好的面试流程是系统化、一致且结构化的。 高管职位的面试流程有时结构良好，但往往并非如此。 如果你用过去面试的经验来应对这些高度个性化的流程，你的直觉可能会误导你。

寻找高管职位时最重要的是记住：虽然有指导原则，有各种故事，甚至有统计数据， 但在寻找高管工作这件事上，并没有绝对的规则。 高管招聘中存在对自信的筛选偏差，这使得你很容易找到一位高管，他会非常自信地告诉你事情是如何运作的，但你要保持一点警惕。 “one of one”意味着一切皆有可能——既有最好的可能，也有最坏的可能。

在公司内部寻找高管职位

很少有人是通过内部晋升获得第一个高管职位的。这类情况很少见有几个原因： 首先，每家公司只有一个工程高管，而且这个职位通常已经有人担任。 其次，公司在寻找新的工程高管时，通常需要这个人具备与现有团队明显不同的技能组合。

即使有人确实在当前公司承担了高管角色，他们也常常难以成功。 他们的挑战与技术主管兼管理角色是个陷阱。的情况类似——既要学习如何做好新工作，又要继续履行之前的职责。 他们还经常要面对之前是自己同事的其他内部候选人，这些人可能因为自己没得到职位而感到被冒犯。 这让新工作变得更加困难，也可能导致关键领导者离职，在公司特别需要稳定的时候掏空组织。

这并不是说你应该回避或拒绝内部晋升为工程高管， 而是说你应该睁大眼睛、充分了解情况再做决定。 从很多方面看，内部转型其实比外部跳槽更难。 即便你在内部经历坎坷，你也完全有资格去另一家公司担任外部高管。

在外部寻找高管职位

大多数高管职位从来不会出现在公司的招聘页面上。 因此，在讨论你该如何进行高管职位搜索之前， 我们先来了解一下公司通常如何为高管职位寻找候选人。 想象一下，我那已经不复存在的公司创立 Monocle Studios如果大获成功，想要招聘第一位CTO，会怎么做。

我们会如何寻找候选人？大概是以下步骤：

  1. 考虑任何内部候选人；
  2. 联系我现有网络中最优秀的人，看看他们是否对这个职位感兴趣；
  3. 让公司内部的高管招聘人员去寻找候选人——如果我们内部没有高管招聘人员，我会跳过这一步，因为高管招聘的网络和方法通常与普通职位招聘不同；
  4. 联系我们现有的投资者寻求帮助，利用他们的网络和他们公司的招聘团队；
  5. 聘请一家高管猎头公司来接管整个搜索工作。

当然并非每家公司每次招聘都严格按照这个方式，但这似乎是普遍一致的做法。 这个结构也解释了为什么“如何找到我的第一个高管职位”这个问题很难回答。 最直接的答案是联系高管猎头，但这种方式也意味着你接触到的职位类型会带有某些特点。 通常这些职位是因为某种原因难以填补。而那些最令人向往的职位，以及由人脉广泛、备受尊敬的CEO亲自负责招聘的职位，往往永远不会流转到猎头公司那里。

顺便说一句，这绝不是建议大家不要使用高管猎头。 高管猎头公司非常优秀。一位好的高管猎头会比普通公司或VC内部招聘人员更认真地指导你完成整个流程。 我自己的第一个高管职位就是通过高管猎头找到的，我大多数同行也是如此——这只是需要记住的一个视角。 同样，并非所有由创始人主导的搜索都是好职位——几乎所有高管职位一开始都是由创始人亲自带领搜索，然后才进入后续流程。

纵观整个招聘流程，在每个环节都有很多方法可以提高你获得高管机会的概率。 基础工作依然重要：维护， 礼貌回复主动联系你的招聘人员。 这两件事都能以惊人的方式制造求职中的意外好运，同时也要让你的网络知道你正在寻找机会。 如果你本人并不认识很多投资机构或高管猎头的招聘人员，那么你的网络在介绍引荐方面会特别有帮助。打造高效的 LinkedIn 高管个人资料

也有少数公司会公开发布高管职位，看看这些也没什么坏处。 唯一的挑战是你需要判断它公开发布是因为公司非常坚持要在自己人脉网络之外寻找人才（这往往是个好信号）， 还是因为这个职位已经走完上面描述的整个漏斗却仍未成功招聘（这往往不是好信号）。

最后，如果你正在为几年后的高管求职做前期准备，其实有很多事情可以做。 你可以加入一家大型或高速增长的公司， 从事能让你接触到公司投资者的工作，， 让创始人更有可能主动联系你，或者积累更多关于如何发展和运营工程组织的相关经验。四十年职业生涯如何成为科技领域的影响者

面试流程

高管职位的面试流程总是有些混乱。 让大多数候选人最惊讶的是，这个流程往往感觉不如他们最近参加的其他面试那么有重点或高效。 这是因为：当你是工程总监时，你的招聘经理通常是一位经验丰富的工程领导者； 但当你是工程高管时，你的招聘经理通常是完全没有工程经验的人。 在前一种情况下，面试你的人非常了解你的工作；而在后一种情况下，面试官通常从未做过这个职位。

当然，例外总是存在的！有时面试官自己曾经担任过工程高管，但这种情况并不常见。 最常见的情况是一位技术型创始人来面试你，可能他继续担任CTO，而你则接手VPE（工程副总裁）的头衔， 但即便如此也值得注意，这个头衔多少有点烟幕弹的意味，他们作为工程高管的经验很可能也有限。

因此，工程高管的面试更多地依赖于感知到的匹配度、个人声望、你之前管理团队的规模、 是否亲切随和，以及如何应对潜在直属下属和同级具体而实际的顾虑。 这使得“细节”在高管面试中尤为重要：快速发送礼貌的跟进邮件， 使用类似来让你的回答简洁有序， 准备能展示你正在强化对公司运作模式理解的问题， 并整体表现出活力和热情。面试问题 STAR 方法

我见过的一般面试流程大致如下：

  • Call with a recruiter to validate you meet the minimum requirements, are a decent communicator, and won’t embarrass them if you talk to the CEO. Also a good opportunity for you to understand whether there are obvious issues that might make this a bad role for you (wrong job location, wrong travel expectations, etc)
  • Call with the CEO or another executive to assess interest in the role, and very high-level potential fit for the role. You’ll be evaluated primarily on your background, your preparation for the discussion, the quality of your communication, and perceived excitement for the company.Coming to this interview having researched the opportunity, and showing excitement for the company, is your biggest leverage point to influence the hiring process
  • Series of CEO or founder discussions where you dig into the business, and their priorities for the role. This will be a mix of you learning from them, them learning about you, and getting a sense of whether you’ll work together well. The exact structure will vary depending on the CEO, and give you an understanding of what kind of person they are to work with
  • One-on-one discussions with a wide smattering of peer executives and members of the team that you would manage. These vary widely across companies, and it is surprisingly common for the interviews to be poorly coordinated, having e.g. the same topics come up multiple times across different interviewers.This is somewhat frustrating. Generally it means the company is missing someone with the right position, experience and energy to invest into designing the loop, so it ends up being rather chaotic. I’ve had these interviews turn into general chats, programming screens, architecture interviews, and anything else you can imagine. All I can say is: roll with it to the best of your ability
  • Presentation interview to executive team, your directors, or a mix of both. Usually you’ll be asked to run a sixty minute presentation describing your background, a point of view on what’s important for the business moving forward, your understanding of what you would focus on in the new role if hired, and your plan for your first ninety days.A few tips that I’ve found effective for these interviews: ask for feedback on your presentation before the session, make sure to follow the prompt directly, prioritize where you want to spend time in the presentation on the highest impact topics, and make sure to leave time for questions (while also having enough bonus content to fill the time if there aren’t many)

如果这听起来出奇地模糊且有些随机，那你理解得没错。 死记硬背标准答案的时代已经过去了。现在的关键是有效地读懂每一个人，并针对他们的视角给出恰当的回应。

合同谈判

一旦公司决定向你发出offer，你就进入了谈判阶段。 虽然谈判的一般规则仍然适用——特别是不要在公司明确表示想录用你之前就开始谈判—— 但在这个时刻，“one of one”的职位特性尤为重要。 薪酬顾问和投资人会给出推荐的薪酬范围，但每家公司每次只招聘一位工程高管，而且每家公司的情况都独一无二。

合理的薪酬会因公司、业务规模、你的所在地以及你自己的背景而有巨大差异。 最好的办法是联系担任类似职位的同行，了解他们的薪酬情况。 我发现人们 surprisingly 愿意分享薪酬细节。 阅读上市公司提交的DEF 14文件也很有帮助，这些文件会说明其最高管理层的底薪、奖金和股权薪酬 （例如，这里是Splunk 2022年的DEF 14A文件）。

这次谈判中有几个方面与以往的薪酬谈判差异足够大，值得专门提出来：

  • Equity is issued in many formats: stock options, Restricted Stock Units, and so on. They are also issued with many conditions: vesting periods (often 4 years), vesting cliffs before vesting accrues (often 1 year), and the duration of the period after you depart when you’re able to exercise options before they expire (often 90 days).Most of these terms are negotiable in an executive offer, but it all comes down to the particular company you’re speaking with. You may be able to negotiate away your vesting cliff, and immediately start vesting monthly rather than waiting a year. You may be able to negotiate an extended post-departure exercise window, even if that isn’t an option more widely. You may be able to have the company issue you a loan to cover your exercise costs, which combined with early exercise might allow you to exercise for “free” except for the very real tax consequences.To determine your negotiation strategy, I highly recommend consulting with a tax advisor, as the “best” option will depend on your particular circumstances
  • Equity acceleration is another negotiation point around equity, that’s worth calling out as it’s common in executive offers, and extremely uncommon in other cases. Acceleration allows you to vest equity immediately if certain conditions are met. Many consider this a standard condition for a startup contract, although there are many executives who don’t have an acceleration clause.One topic that gets perhaps undue attention is the distinction between single and double trigger acceleration. “Single trigger” acceleration has only one condition to be met (for example, your company is acquired), whereas “double trigger” acceleration will specify two conditions (for example, your company is acquired and you lose your job). My sense is that people like to talk about single and double triggers because it makes them sound knowledgable about the topic rather than it being a particularly nuanced aspect of the discussion.
  • Severance packages can be negotiated, guaranteeing compensation after you exit the role. There is little consistency on this topic. Agreements range from executives at very small companies that’ve pre-negotiated a few months salary as a severance package, to executives leaving highly compensated roles that require their new company to make them whole on the compensation they’re leaving behind. There are also many executive contracts that don’t pre-negotiate severance at all, leaving the negotiation until the departure (when you admittedly have limited leverage to work with)
  • Bonus size and calculation can be negotiated. On average, bonus tends to be a larger component of roles outside of engineering, such as a sales executive, but like everything, this is company and size specific. A CTO at a public company might have their bonus be equal in size to their salary. A CTO at a Series C company might have a 20% bonus. A CTO at a 50 person company might have no bonus at all.In addition to the size of your bonus, you may be able to negotiate the conditions for earning it. This won’t matter with companies that rely on a shared bonus goal for all executives (sometimes excluding sales), but may matter a great deal at others that employ bespoke, per-executive goals instead
  • Parental leave can be negotiated. For example, some companies might only offer paid parental leave after a year of service, but you can absolutely negotiate to include that after a shorter amount of service. (It’s worth noting, this is often negotiable in less senior roles as well.)
  • Start date is generally quite easy to negotiate in less senior roles, but can be unexpectedly messy for executive roles. The reason it gets messy is that the hiring company often has an urgent need for the role to be filled, while also wanting to see a great deal of excitement from the candidate about joining.The quiet part is that many recruiters and companies have seen executive candidates accept but later not join due to an opposing offer being sweetened, which makes them uncomfortable delaying, particularly for candidates who have been negotiating with other companies, including their current one
  • Support to perform your role successfully. The typical example of vain requests for support are guaranteed business or first-class seats on business travel, but there are other dimensions of support that will genuinely impact your ability to perform your role. For example, an 如何与执行助理合作 can free up hours every week for focus work, and sufficient budget to staff your team can easily be the difference between a great and terrible first year.The negotiation phase is the right time to ask for whatever you’ll need to succeed in the role. You’ll never have an easier time to ensure you and your organization can succeed

谈判时要知道一切皆有可能，但也要记住，谈判结束后你还要和这些和你谈判的人一起共事。 如果你过多地施压，你不会是第一个因为公司对你是否真的想加入失去信心而被撤回offer的候选人。

决定是否接受这份工作

一旦你收到高管职位的offer，要拒绝它可能会变得异常困难。 和你合作的招聘人员会推动你接受。 和你沟通的公司会推动你接受。 你自己也在这个流程中投入了大量精力，这也会让你倾向于接受。

评估一份高管offer也很有挑战性，因为你实质上在做两件非常困难的事情。 首先，你要预测公司未来的发展轨迹，这即使对经常做这件事的风险投资人也非常困难 （而且他们解决的是一个更容易的问题，因为他们可以同时投很多家公司，而你一次只能接受一份工作）。 其次，你要做出一个能平衡自己所有需求的决定，而令人惊讶的是，有相当多的人在这件事上判断失误 （包括接受他们知道自己会讨厌的 prestigious 或高薪职位，却就是无法说不）。

我无法告诉你是否应该接受offer，但我会强烈建议你在最终决定前完成以下几个步骤：

  • Spend enough time with the CEO to be sure you’ll enjoy working with them, and trust them to lead the company. While it changes a bit as companies scale, and particularly as they go public, the CEO is the person who will be deciding company direction, determining the members of the executive team, and responsible to resolve the trickiest decisions
  • Speak to at least one member of their board. Admittedly, board members won’t directly tell you anything too spicy, but their willingness to meet with you is an important signal, and it’s the best opportunity to start building your relationship with the board
  • Make sure you’ve spoken with every member of the executive team that you’d work with regularly. Sometimes you’ll miss someone in your process due to scheduling issues, and it’s important to chat with everyone and make sure they’re folks you can build an effective working relationship with
  • Have someone on the finance team to walk you through the company’s most recent 解读损益表. You will need to sign a Non Disclosure Agreement, but it would be a very odd sign if a company was unwilling to share this information with you
  • Make sure they’ve actually answered your questions. I once interviewed to be a company’s head of engineering, where they refused to share their current valuation with me! I pushed a few times, but ultimately they told me it was unreasonable to ask, and I decided I couldn’t move forward with a company that wouldn’t even share their valuation with an executive candidate.Don’t assume they’ll tell you after you join if they won’t tell you when trying to convince you to accept their offer. You will never have more leverage to get questions answered than in the hiring process: if it’s important and they won’t answer, be willing to walk away
  • If the company has recently had executives depart, see if you can get an understanding for why. This could be mutual friends with the departed executive, or even chatting with them directly

当你完成这些步骤后，如果你仍然感到兴奋？你是否已经向至少两位朋友解释过你对这个职位的思考，而他们没有提出任何严重担忧？ 那么，一般来说，接受这份工作吧！

没有得到这份工作

最后，谈论如何进行工程高管职位搜索，就不能不谈没有得到工作的情况。 谁没有被猎头联系、初筛后就被ghosted的故事呢？ 一家上市公司最近邀请我的一位朋友参加他们的CTO招聘流程，让朋友非常兴奋，结果第二周就通知他说他们已经暂时填补了这个职位。 我曾经和一些CEO进行第一次谈话，双方立刻就知道我们不适合一起工作。

虽然被拒绝并不好受，但我发现有帮助的视角是：你搜索的目标不是找到一份高管工作， 而是找到一份你能在其中茁壮成长的高管工作。 在接受职位之前就意识到它不适合你，要好得多。每一个没有继续推进的机会，其实都是最好的结果。
